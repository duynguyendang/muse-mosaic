The Mission

Before an agent can act, it needs clean data.

Sending an OTP to a wrong number or refunding the wrong account happens when agents act on raw, messy fragments. Mosaic guarantees clean records with verifiable proof—or tells downstream to halt.

Strict Core Invariant:
Muse proposes · Datalog decides · Publisher writes
The 4 Pressures of Enterprise Data Linkage
01 · VOLUME 📈

Many (Pairwise $O(N^2)$)

Comparing every record naively explodes: 2.5k records is already 3.3M pairs; portfolios reach trillions.

02 · REPRESENTATION 🔀

Messy (Syntax & Diacritics)

Differing keys (sdt, dien_thoai), nicknames (Bob $\leftrightarrow$ Robert), and transposed name orders.

03 · AUTHORITY ⚖️

Multi-Source Conflicts

CRM, ERP, and tickets disagree. Tickets hold unverified data that cannot be trusted without a provenance gate.

04 · VELOCITY ⚡

Fast (Sub-second Deciding)

Traditional MDM takes months of consulting and heavy setup. Teams need batch linkage in seconds, not weeks.

The 3 Independent Pillars & The Trust Gate
Pillar 1 · Scale :8081

Splink (Matching Engine)

Fast candidate clustering via DuckDB. Blocks on identifiers (phone, email, order ID) to reduce billions of pairs to candidate clusters in seconds.

Role: Proposer Only Narrows search space. Never publishes or decides a winner.
Pillar 2 · Understanding :8000

Muse (LLM Semantic Judge)

Zero-shot semantic reasoning over hard cases (nicknames, initials, Vietnamese diacritics). Operates per cluster on bounded shortlists—never 1 giant prompt.

Role: Semantic Claims Zero false merges on 10k benchmark. Sends hint labels only, no raw PII.
Pillar 3 · Proof & Gate :8080

Manglekit (Go + Datalog)

The sole deterministic decider. Enforces mechanical link proofs (S1 phone, S2 email), authority tiers (R1), and dispute guards (R2, S3).

Role: Deterministic Decider Pure logic. Refuses unverified claims. Publisher validates whole schema.
